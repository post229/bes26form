New to BES?

This is a clinical medical summit for licensed professionals — not a general beauty event. Expect practical, case-based content across three specialities.

Common questions

Can I attend online?

Yes — online access includes live streaming of both days, an interactive chat, a web certificate, and 30 days of recordings.

Will I get a certificate?

Every registered participant receives a certificate of attendance. CME/CPD accreditation has been submitted and will be confirmed.

Is there a group discount?

Groups of 5 or more receive a 15% discount — mention this in the additional information step.

Can I get a visa invitation letter?

Yes — request one in the Visa & Official Invitation step. Letters are issued within 3–5 business days for visa-support purposes.
